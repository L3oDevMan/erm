Directions

The questions in this section cover a range of math skills. You may use a calculator on every question, and a reference sheet of common formulas is available from the Reference button.

Unless a question says otherwise, all variables and expressions stand for real numbers, figures are drawn to scale and lie in a plane, and the domain of a function f is every real number x for which f(x) is a real number.

For multiple-choice questions, work out the answer and pick the one best choice. For student-produced response questions, type your answer in the box; the rules for entering it are shown next to each of those questions.

Student-produced response directions
  • Enter only one answer, even if you find more than one that works.
  • A positive answer can use up to 5 characters; a negative answer up to 6 (the minus sign counts as one).
  • Fractions and decimals are both fine. If a fraction is too long for the box, enter its decimal instead.
  • If a decimal is too long, round it or cut it off at the fourth digit (so it fills the box).
  • Don't enter mixed numbers: write 3½ as 7/2 or 3.5.
  • Leave out symbols such as %, $, commas and units.
