Directions

The questions in this section cover a range of math skills. You may use a calculator on every question, and the calculator, reference sheet and these directions stay available for the whole module.

Unless a question says otherwise, all variables and expressions stand for real numbers, figures are drawn to scale and lie in a plane, and a function's domain is every real number for which it gives a real value.

Multiple-choice questions: choose the one correct answer from the four choices.
